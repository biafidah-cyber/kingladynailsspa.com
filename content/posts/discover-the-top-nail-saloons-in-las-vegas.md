## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the benefits of gel nails?

Gel nails offer a sleek, natural look and are more durable than regular polish. They last longer, making them ideal for busy lifestyles.

### How often should I get a manicure?

It's advisable to get a manicure every two to three weeks to maintain nail health and aesthetics.

### Are acrylic nails safe?

Yes, when applied and removed correctly by professionals, acrylic nails are safe. Ensure the salon follows proper procedures.

### What should I avoid after a manicure?

Avoid activities that may chip the polish, such as heavy cleaning or using harsh chemicals. Moisturize your hands regularly.

### How can I ensure a salon is hygienic?

Check for clean tools, sterilized equipment, and overall cleanliness of the salon environment.
